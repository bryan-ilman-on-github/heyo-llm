# Heyo

Heyo is a personal AI you talk to like a close friend. You just type whatever’s on your mind. It remembers your life, connects past moments, and helps you think clearly when you forget, panic, or feel stuck.

Not a chatbot. Not a planner. Not therapy. More like someone who actually knows you and stays with you over time.

## What it does

* Lives in one continuous chat
* Quietly remembers context from your life
* Connects past moments with what you are dealing with now
* Helps you think faster and clearer
* Reduces repeating, explaining, rewriting, or keeping notes
* Preps you for real life decisions, meetings, and conversations

## Why it matters

Your brain is loaded with unfinished thoughts. Heyo keeps the backlog, links it together, and hands it back when it counts.

## One-liner

An AI friend that remembers your life so you can think better.
